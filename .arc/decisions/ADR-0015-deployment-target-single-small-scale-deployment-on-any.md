## Context

Developer: 'Small scale, single hotel traffic (tens-hundreds of bookings/day); standard business-hours availability is fine, nightly backups sufficient' and 'No constraint — pick any mainstream cloud provider/region, standard small-business budget'.

## Decision

Single small-scale deployment on any mainstream cloud provider/region (no mandated provider), sized for tens-hundreds of bookings/day, no HA/multi-region requirement, standard small-business budget
